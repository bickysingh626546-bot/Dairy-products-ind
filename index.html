Heritage & Mission

From 12 Sahiwal Cows to 15,000+ Happy Households

Lucky Dairy Products began as a humble family initiative founded by Shri Lakhwinder Singh in 1988 with an unwavering vow: never sell milk that our own grandchildren wouldn't drink unboiled with joy.

Today, we combine timeless pastoral wisdom — like slow hand-bilona curd churning for Desi Ghee and natural clay-matka dahi setting — with cutting-edge chilling cold-chain logistics across Delhi-NCR and Tricity.
